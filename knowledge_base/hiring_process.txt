NIMBUS TECH - HIRING PROCESS
Owner: Talent Acquisition Team
Last reviewed: March 2026

OVERVIEW
Our hiring process has 6 stages. The average time from application to offer is 21 days.

STAGE 1 - JOB REQUISITION
The hiring manager raises a requisition in the HR portal with the role, level and budget.
The department head and the finance team must approve the requisition before it is published.

STAGE 2 - SOURCING
Roles are posted on the company careers page, LinkedIn and two job boards.
Employee referrals are encouraged. A referral bonus of 20,000 INR is paid after the new hire completes 6 months.

STAGE 3 - RESUME SCREENING
Recruiters shortlist resumes within 5 working days of the application.
Shortlisting is based on skills, relevant experience and the job description.

STAGE 4 - INTERVIEW ROUNDS
Candidates go through a recruiter call, a technical interview, and a managerial interview.
Senior roles also have a system design round and a culture-fit round with a director.
Every interviewer fills in a scorecard within 24 hours of the interview.

STAGE 5 - OFFER AND BACKGROUND CHECK
The offer is approved by HR and finance. The offer letter is sent within 3 working days of the final decision.
Candidates have 7 days to accept the offer.
A background check covering education, employment history and identity is done before the joining date.

STAGE 6 - JOINING
The standard notice period for new joiners is 60 days.
HR sends the pre-joining kit one week before the joining date.

DIVERSITY
We use structured interviews and the same scorecard for every candidate to keep hiring fair.
