NIMBUS TECH - TECHNICAL INTERVIEW GUIDE
Audience: Interviewers and candidates for engineering roles

FORMAT
The technical interview lasts 60 minutes and is held on a video call.
It is split into 5 minutes of introduction, 35 minutes of problem solving, 15 minutes of discussion and 5 minutes for candidate questions.

CODING ROUND
Candidates solve 2 problems in a language of their choice. Python, Java and JavaScript are the most common.
Problems test data structures such as arrays, hash maps, stacks, queues and trees.
Candidates are expected to explain their approach before writing code.
Time and space complexity should be discussed for every solution.
Running the code is optional, but candidates should dry-run it with a sample input.

SYSTEM DESIGN ROUND (SENIOR ROLES)
Candidates design a system such as a URL shortener, a chat application or a notification service.
Topics include APIs, databases, caching, load balancing and handling failures.
Interviewers look for clear trade-offs, not one perfect answer.

AI AND DATA ROLES
Candidates may be asked about embeddings, vector search and how large language models use tools.
A basic question is the difference between a model's training data and the context it receives at run time.

SCORING
Each interviewer scores problem solving, code quality, communication and technical depth from 1 to 5.
A candidate needs an average of at least 3.5 to move to the next round.

TIPS FOR CANDIDATES
Ask clarifying questions before you start coding.
Think out loud, so the interviewer can follow your reasoning.
It is fine to say you do not know something. Do not guess without saying so.
Keep your camera on and test your internet connection before the call.
