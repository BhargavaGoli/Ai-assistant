NIMBUS TECH - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Owner: Finance Team

SCOPE
This policy covers business expenses such as travel, meals, accommodation and client meetings.

GENERAL RULES
Only expenses that are needed for business and approved by your manager are reimbursed.
Keep the original bill or receipt for every expense above 200 INR.
Submit your claim in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.
Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.

TRAVEL
Flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel desk, at least 7 days in advance.
Train travel in AC 2-tier is allowed for journeys under 12 hours.
Local travel by taxi or auto is reimbursed with the receipt or the app invoice.
Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at 9 INR per kilometre.

ACCOMMODATION
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 6,000 INR per night in metro cities.
In other cities the limit is 4,000 INR per night.

MEALS
The daily meal allowance during travel is 1,200 INR.
Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances.

CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT
Client meals need prior approval from the department head.
Include the names of the guests and the business purpose in the claim.

PHONE AND INTERNET
Employees whose roles need it can claim up to 800 INR per month for their mobile bill.

APPROVAL AND PAYMENT
Manager approval is needed first, then the finance team checks the claim.
Approved claims are paid with the salary of the following month.
Claims above 50,000 INR need approval from the finance director.

FALSE CLAIMS
Submitting a false or inflated claim is a serious breach of the code of conduct.
