NIMBUS TECH - REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
Effective from: 1 April 2026

PURPOSE
This policy explains how employees can work from home or from another approved location.

WORK MODELS
Hybrid: 3 days in the office and 2 days remote each week. This is the default model.
Fully remote: available only for roles approved by the department head and HR.
Office-based: required for roles that need lab or hardware access.

CORE HOURS
All employees must be available between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM India Standard Time.
Outside core hours, employees can arrange their schedule with their manager.
Standard working time is 8 hours a day, including a one-hour lunch break.

EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCE
The company provides a laptop and a monitor to every employee.
Remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 INR.
A monthly internet allowance of 1,000 INR is paid to employees working remotely at least 2 days a week.

SECURITY RULES
Always connect to the company VPN when accessing internal systems from home.
Do not work on company data from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
Lock your screen when you step away from your laptop.
Report a lost or stolen device to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.

WORKING FROM ANOTHER CITY OR COUNTRY
Working from another city within India for up to 30 days a year needs manager approval.
Working from another country needs approval from HR and legal, due to tax and compliance rules.

MEETINGS
Keep your camera on during team meetings unless your manager says otherwise.
Update your status in Slack so that your team knows when you are available.
