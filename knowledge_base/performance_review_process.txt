NIMBUS TECH - PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS
Owner: People Operations

REVIEW CYCLE
Performance reviews are held twice a year: a mid-year check-in in July and an annual review in January.
The annual review decides salary increases, bonuses and promotions.

STEP 1 - SET GOALS
At the start of the year, each employee agrees on 3 to 5 goals with their manager.
Goals should be specific, measurable and linked to team targets.
Goals are recorded in the HR portal.

STEP 2 - SELF ASSESSMENT
Two weeks before the review, employees write a self assessment.
They list achievements, challenges and the skills they want to grow.

STEP 3 - PEER FEEDBACK
Each employee chooses 3 peers to give feedback.
Peer feedback is anonymous and is shared with the manager only as a summary.

STEP 4 - MANAGER REVIEW
The manager rates the employee on a scale of 1 to 5.
1 means needs improvement, 3 means meets expectations and 5 means outstanding.
Ratings are calibrated across the department to keep them fair.

STEP 5 - REVIEW MEETING
The manager and employee hold a one-hour meeting to discuss the rating and the feedback.
Employees can add comments in the portal if they disagree with the rating.

SALARY INCREASE AND BONUS
Employees rated 3 receive an average increase of 8 percent.
Employees rated 4 or 5 receive a higher increase and are eligible for the annual performance bonus.
Increases take effect from 1 April.

PROMOTIONS
An employee is considered for promotion after at least 18 months in their current level.
Promotions need a written case from the manager and approval from the department head.

PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN
An employee rated 1 is put on a 90-day improvement plan with clear goals and weekly check-ins.
