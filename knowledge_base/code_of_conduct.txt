NIMBUS TECH - CODE OF CONDUCT
Applies to: All employees, contractors and interns

OUR VALUES
We act with integrity, treat people with respect, and take ownership of our work.

RESPECT AT WORK
We do not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind.
This includes discrimination based on gender, age, religion, caste, disability, sexual orientation or nationality.
Jokes, messages or images that make a colleague uncomfortable are not acceptable, online or offline.

CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must tell HR if they have a personal or financial interest that could affect their work.
Working for a competitor or a client at the same time needs written approval from HR.

GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
Gifts worth more than 2,000 INR from a vendor or client must be declined or reported to the compliance team.
Never accept cash or cash equivalents as a gift.

CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Do not share customer data, source code or internal documents with anyone outside the company.
This duty continues after you leave the company.
Do not paste confidential code or customer data into public AI tools.

USE OF COMPANY RESOURCES
Company laptops and accounts are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work.

REPORTING CONCERNS
Employees can report a concern to their manager, to HR or to the ethics hotline at ethics@nimbustech.example.
Reports can be made anonymously. The company does not allow retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

CONSEQUENCES
Breaking this code can lead to a warning, suspension or termination, depending on how serious it is.
Illegal acts will be reported to the authorities.
