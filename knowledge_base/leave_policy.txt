NIMBUS TECH - LEAVE POLICY
Effective from: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All full-time employees

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at 1.5 days per month of service.
Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.
Carried forward leave expires on 31 March of the following year.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

3. CASUAL LEAVE
Employees get 6 days of casual leave per year for urgent personal matters.
Casual leave can be taken for a maximum of 2 consecutive days at a time.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for up to two children.
Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
Adoption leave of 12 weeks is available to the primary caregiver.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees get 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member.
Immediate family means spouse, children, parents, siblings and grandparents.

6. HOW TO APPLY
Apply for leave through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.
Leave longer than 5 consecutive days needs approval from the department head.
Emergency leave can be applied for within 24 hours of returning to work.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published every December.
Employees can choose 2 optional holidays from a list of 6 regional festivals.

8. UNPAID LEAVE
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days is possible with manager and HR approval.
Salary is deducted on a pro-rata basis for each unpaid day.
