NIMBUS TECH - NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Owner: People Operations

BEFORE DAY ONE
You will receive a welcome email 5 days before your joining date.
Please submit your ID proof, address proof, bank details and two passport photos through the HR portal.
Your laptop is shipped to your home address if you are joining as a remote employee.

DAY ONE
Orientation starts at 10:00 AM in the main conference room, or on video for remote joiners.
You will meet your manager, your onboarding buddy and the HR partner for your team.
IT will set up your company email, your VPN access and your single sign-on account.
You will receive your employee ID card and an access card for the office.

FIRST WEEK
Complete the mandatory security awareness training within 5 working days.
Read the code of conduct, the leave policy and the remote work policy.
Set up your development environment using the engineering handbook.
Your buddy will walk you through the codebase and the team's tools.

FIRST 30 DAYS
Meet your manager every week for a one-to-one meeting.
Agree on your 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.
Complete your first small task or bug fix, and get it reviewed and merged.

PROBATION
The probation period is 6 months for all new employees.
A probation review is held at the end of month 3 and again at month 6.
Employees are confirmed in writing after a successful probation review.

TOOLS WE USE
Slack for team chat, Jira for tracking work, Confluence for documents and GitHub for source code.

WHO TO ASK FOR HELP
For IT problems, raise a ticket on the IT helpdesk. For HR questions, email hr@nimbustech.example.
